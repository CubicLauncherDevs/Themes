# Frieren Aureole

A clean theme inspired by Sousou no Frieren and the editorial design of Chinese landing pages: asymmetrical composition, white space, serif headlines, and botanical details. The wallpaper retains its original colors. Warm ivory, sage, and forest green complement the landscape; small gold accents evoke Frieren’s staff.

SVG icons by Lucide: book, settings controls, pen, flower, and hourglass. Brief animations, with no loops or blurring. Vendor logos and loaders retain their original appearance.

## Installation

Import Frieren_Aureole.cbth from the CubicLauncher themes section and select Frieren Aureole. No need to extract the package.

## Compatibility

V2 format, tested with the importer from the “develop” branch as of September 10, 2026. Meta.toml uses fields at the root; Definition.toml uses [colors], [background], [icons.nav], etc., without wrapping them in [theme]. Some examples in the guide show wrappers that the separate file importer does not use.

The TOML structure, paths, and package resources were validated. The visual review in the browser could not be performed, and the import was not tested on Tauri. Versions with other components may require selector adjustments.
